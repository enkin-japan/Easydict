Dictionary data bundled with Easydict Slim
==========================================

builtin-dictionary.sqlite.xz is generated by scripts/builtin-dict/update.sh from the
following data. The generated database is a derivative of these works and is distributed
under the same terms.

English - Chinese
  ECDICT - https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT
  Copyright (c) 2025 Linwei. MIT License (below).

Japanese - English
  JMdict and KANJIDIC2, the property of the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development
  Group (EDRDG, https://www.edrdg.org/), used in conformance with the Group's licence:
  Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
  Converted to JSON by jmdict-simplified (https://github.com/scriptin/jmdict-simplified).
  Example sentences come from the Tatoeba project (https://tatoeba.org/), licensed under
  Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/).

Japanese pitch accent
  Kanjium - https://github.com/mifunetoshiro/kanjium
  Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
  "The pitch accent notation, verb particle data, phonetics, homonyms and other additions
  or modifications to EDICT, KANJIDIC or KRADFILE were provided by Uros O. through his
  free database."

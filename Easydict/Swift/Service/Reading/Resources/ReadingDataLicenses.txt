Reading dictionaries bundled with Easydict Slim
================================================

The files zh-phrases.tsv.lzfse, zh-characters.tsv.lzfse and ja-furigana.tsv.lzfse
are generated by scripts/reading-dict/update.sh from the following data.

zh-phrases.tsv.lzfse
  phrase-pinyin-data (large_pinyin.txt) - https://github.com/mozillazg/phrase-pinyin-data
  Copyright (c) 2017 mozillazg. MIT License (below).
  Includes pinyin data derived from CC-CEDICT (https://cc-cedict.org/,
  Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International).

zh-characters.tsv.lzfse
  pinyin-data (pinyin.txt) - https://github.com/mozillazg/pinyin-data
  Copyright (c) 2016 mozillazg. MIT License (below).

ja-furigana.tsv.lzfse
  JmdictFurigana - https://github.com/Doublevil/JmdictFurigana
  Copyright (c) 2025 Doublevil. MIT License (below).
  Built from the JMdict dictionary file, the property of the Electronic Dictionary
  Research and Development Group (EDRDG, https://www.edrdg.org/), used in conformance
